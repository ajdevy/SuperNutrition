# G3 component-model selection — superseded

**Status:** withdrawn from current product decisions on 2026-11-02.

The earlier detector selection was superseded by repository-owner decisions
D-11 and D-13. Its model integration and model-specific artifacts were removed
from the SuperNutrition project and its reachable branch/tag history. No
component or mass model is currently selected. Food-101 remains a
dish-classification baseline and does not identify ingredients or estimate
grams.

G-3 remains open until a separately cleared model, consented weighed corpus,
physical evidence, and the required product/engineering approval bundle exist.
